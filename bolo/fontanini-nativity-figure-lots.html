ITALIAN NATIVITY COLLECTIBLES · PRICES CHECKED OCTOBER 9, 2026

Fontanini nativity figure lots: scale and markings matter

Conservative price signal: Eight recent Fontanini lots averaged $35.58 before shipping, with a reviewed range of $14.21–$65.00.

Sample: 8 completed eBay sales from Oct. 4–9, 2026, covering 3–12 marked figures. An unbranded “Made in Italy” lot and a $150 fourteen-piece large-scale set were excluded.

Exact identification cues

Look underneath each figure for Fontanini, Roman, Depose Italy or Italy markings and, when present, a model number or date. Measure the standing adult figure: common 5-inch-scale pieces should be grouped with the same scale. The flexible polymer/resin feel and detailed hand-painted faces distinguish many Fontanini figures from brittle ceramic copies.

Condition and grouping

Check fingers, staffs, animal ears, horns and base edges. Light paint rub is common; chewing, deep bends, splits and missing accessories lower value. Group matching-scale people separately from animals, and call out named or numbered figures because scarce characters may deserve individual listings.

Buy and listing plan

Try to pay no more than: $12–$18 for a mixed marked lot of 5–8 ordinary pieces
Suggested listing price: $39.99–$59.99 after checking names and scale
Quick-sale target: $30–$40

Displayed item prices exclude shipping. Lot size, scale and exact characters vary, so the average is a screening signal—not a per-figure price.

Shipping plan

Wrap every figure separately, protect projecting staffs and animal horns with soft support, and prevent contact between pieces. Bag any loose accessories. A compact rigid box with two inches of cushioning is safer than a padded mailer.

Main risks

Generic Italian figures are often misidentified as Fontanini. Mixed scales, unmarked pieces and common animals can dilute a lot. Conversely, rare named figures or complete large-scale sets can be outliers and should be researched one by one.

Sold history is a snapshot, not a guarantee. Re-check markings, scale, condition, fees and shipping before buying.
