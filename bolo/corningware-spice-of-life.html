KITCHEN AISLE · PRICES CHECKED SEPTEMBER 25, 2026

CorningWare Spice of Life: realistic resale numbers

Conservative price signal: Four clear fixed-price or auction examples averaged about $15 before shipping, with ordinary pieces ranging roughly $7.50–$34.95. Large lidded pieces can do better; common small dishes are modest flips.

Sample: 4 recent sold listings with visible fixed or auction prices, Sep. 23–24, 2026.

Exact identification cues

Look for cream-white glass-ceramic with vegetables, herbs and French names such as L’Echalote, Le Romarin or La Marjolaine. Flip the dish and record the model code (for example A-1-B, A-10-B or P-43-B), capacity and whether the lid is correct.

Condition and testing

Inspect handles and rims for chips, the base for cracks, the pattern for wear, and the lid for flea bites. Heavy baked-on residue and replacement lids reduce value. Photograph the backstamp and model code.

Buy and listing plan

Target buy ceiling: $5 for common singles; up to $12 for a large clean piece with correct lid
Suggested list price: $24.99–$39.99 depending on size and lid
Quick-sale target: $14.99–$24.99

Shipping plan

Double-box larger roasters. Separate lid from dish with cardboard and bubble wrap, protect handles, and allow at least 2 inches of cushioning on every side.

Main risks

Online myths exaggerate value. Exact model, lid and size matter; shipping damage can erase the margin. Avoid pricing from rare asking-price headlines.

Sold history is a market snapshot, not a guarantee. Re-check exact model, condition, fees, and shipping before buying.
