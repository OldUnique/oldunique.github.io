HOLIDAY TEXTILES · PRICES CHECKED OCTOBER 2, 2026

1987 Avon mouse advent calendars: completeness doubles the story

Conservative price signal: Six recent complete examples with the original mouse averaged $108.40 before shipping. Calendar-only examples were commonly around $49–$65, so verify the mouse before paying up.

Sample: 6 complete calendar-and-mouse sales from Sep. 30–Oct. 1, 2026; no-mouse examples and a $204.99 never-used premium outlier excluded.

Exact identification cues

The rectangular fabric wall hanging reads “Countdown to Christmas” and has 24 numbered red pockets, a Santa scene, hanging cord or dowel, and a small white plush mouse that moves pocket to pocket. Many listings date it to 1987. Confirm the mouse is original and not a replacement ornament.

Condition and testing

Check every pocket seam, the top hanging channel or dowel, cord, embroidery and lower edge for stains, tears or odors. Inspect the mouse for yellowing, missing facial details and loose stuffing. Photograph the date or Avon label if present.

Buy and listing plan

Try to pay no more than: $35 complete with the original mouse; $15–$20 without it
Suggested listing price: $129.99–$149.99 complete and clean
Quick-sale target: $99.99–$114.99

Shipping plan

Do not sharply crease the embroidered face. Roll loosely around clean tissue or fold only along natural seams, bag against moisture, and protect the dowel separately. The plush mouse should be bagged so it cannot snag a pocket.

Main risks

The mouse is frequently missing and is a major share of the value. Musty odor, storage staining and replaced hanging hardware should be disclosed. A never-used $204.99 result was excluded as a premium outlier.
