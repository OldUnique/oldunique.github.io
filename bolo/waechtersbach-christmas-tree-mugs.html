VINTAGE HOLIDAY TABLEWARE · PRICES CHECKED OCTOBER 5, 2026

Waechtersbach Christmas-tree mugs: sell clean matching sets

Conservative price signal: Five recent four-mug sets averaged $25.63 before shipping. Singles can bring roughly $19 shipped, but matching groups of four are easier to merchandise and justify careful packing.

Sample: 5 four-mug Christmas-tree sets sold Sep. 21–30, 2026; singles, pairs, six- and eight-mug sets, alternate animal or house patterns and paper gift bags excluded.

Exact identification cues

The common keeper is a glossy red stoneware mug with a simple green Christmas tree and small white accents. Check the underside for Waechtersbach and Germany or West Germany markings. Match handle shape, height, shade of red and backstamp before calling a group a set.

Condition and testing

Inspect rims and handles under strong light for flea bites and hairlines, then check the red glaze for utensil marks, crazing and dishwasher haze. Run a finger around each rim and photograph every backstamp. Do not combine different heights or patterns merely because all are red.

Buy and listing plan

Suggested listing price: $34.99–$39.99 plus shipping
Quick-sale target: $24.99–$29.99

Shipping plan

Wrap every mug separately, protect handles with extra folded padding, and keep handles from facing one another. Use dividers or snug void fill in a sturdy box; double-box when the set is especially clean or the destination is distant.

Main risks

Small rim chips can hide in the dark glaze, and mixed generations may look mismatched in person. Shipping cost can erase profit on low-dollar sets, so weigh the packed box before listing. Treat unusually high single-mug results as pattern- or shipping-driven, not the baseline.
