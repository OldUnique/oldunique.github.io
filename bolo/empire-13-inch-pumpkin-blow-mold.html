Empire 13-inch pumpkin blow molds: a realistic Halloween flip

Conservative price signal: Six recent clear fixed-price sales averaged $29.24 before shipping. The ordinary 13-inch pumpkin is a modest flip; scarecrow faces and unusual variants can sell higher.

Sample: 6 fixed-price sales from Sep. 17–27, 2026; best-offer and scarecrow variants excluded.

Exact identification cues

Look for an orange molded-plastic jack-o-lantern about 13 inches tall, a rear bulb opening and an Empire Plastics or 1968 marking molded into the base or back. Separate the ordinary pumpkin face from scarecrow, witch-hat and double-sided variants.

Condition and testing

Confirm the cord, socket and bulb work safely. Inspect for splits around the bulb opening, melted plastic, crushed walls, faded orange color, marker, paint loss and replacement cords. Never leave an old light unattended while testing.

Buy and listing plan

Try to pay no more than: $8 for a clean working ordinary pumpkin; $3–$5 if untested
Suggested listing price: $34.99–$39.99 plus calculated shipping
Quick-sale target: $24.99–$29.99

Shipping plan

Remove the bulb, wrap it separately, and prevent the cord from rubbing the plastic. Use a box with room around the entire mold; do not compress the thin walls.

Main risks

The large shipping box can erase a small margin. Scarecrow and rare face variants are not direct comps for the common pumpkin. Old electrical parts may need replacement.
