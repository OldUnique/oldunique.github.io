VINTAGE CHRISTMAS LIGHTS · PRICES CHECKED OCTOBER 2, 2026

GE Merry Midget tinsel lights: the sleeves are the signal

Conservative price signal: Four complete working 20-light tinsel sets averaged $36.24 before shipping. Ordinary bare Merry Midget strings sell for less; the intact metallic tinsel reflectors and original box are the value cue.

Sample: 4 working boxed tinsel sets sold Jul. 9–Aug. 20, 2026; a two-set lot marked “READ” and replacement-parts listings excluded.

Exact identification cues

Look for a General Electric or GE Merry Midget box describing 20 lights, plus individual metallic tinsel reflector sleeves around the miniature bulbs. Match the boxed set rather than ordinary 20-, 35- or 50-light bare strings. Original cardboard inserts and the correct small two-pin bulbs strengthen the match.

Condition and testing

Test the entire strand for at least ten minutes. Inspect old wire for stiffness, cracks, exposed copper or heat damage; confirm every socket works. Count all 20 tinsel sleeves and note crushing, oxidation, missing bulbs or substituted modern parts.

Buy and listing plan

Try to pay no more than: $10 for a complete working set; $5 or less if untested
Suggested listing price: $44.99–$54.99 boxed and tested
Quick-sale target: $29.99–$39.99

Shipping plan

Coil the strand loosely without bending old wire sharply. Cushion the box inside a larger shipping carton so the cardboard and tinsel sleeves cannot crush.

Main risks

A working vintage light set is still old electrical equipment. Do not promise long-term safety. Missing tinsel sleeves, brittle wire and ordinary bare-light variants can cut the value sharply.
