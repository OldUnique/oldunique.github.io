VINTAGE CHRISTMAS WAX FIGURES · PRICES CHECKED OCTOBER 9, 2026

Vintage Gurley Christmas candle lots: labels, condition and grouping

Conservative price signal: Six recent mixed Christmas lots averaged $24.17 before shipping, with a reviewed range of $12.60–$36.99.

Sample: 6 completed eBay sales from Oct. 1–8, 2026. Single figures, unusually large lots, premium boxed sets and obvious non-Gurley assortments were excluded.

Exact identification cues

Look on the base for the paper Gurley Novelty Co. label, often naming Buffalo, New York and showing the original low retail price. Common Christmas forms include choir children, Santa, snowmen, angels, trees, deer, lampposts and small village pieces. Vintage wax should have molded details and hand-painted accents; modern “Gurley inspired” candles are not comparable.

Condition and grouping

Prioritize unburned wicks, straight figures, intact labels and clean paint. Heat causes leaning, flattening and wax bloom; cracks, broken accessories and soot lower value. Build coherent lots of four to eight by holiday, scale or character family. Photograph every base label and disclose any candle that will not stand straight.

Buy and listing plan

Try to pay no more than: $8–$10 for a clean mixed lot of 4–8
Suggested listing price: $29.99–$39.99 for a cohesive labeled lot
Quick-sale target: $22–$28

Shipping plan

Wrap each figure in acid-free tissue, then bubble wrap without pressing fragile wax details. Separate pieces with cardboard cells, keep the parcel away from heat, and avoid leaving packed candles in a hot vehicle. Use a rigid box with no movement.

Main risks

Reproductions, missing labels, melted or leaning wax, mixed-maker lots and private offer prices can distort comparisons. Rare boxed Santa-and-reindeer sets may sell far above the ordinary mixed-lot range and should be researched separately.
