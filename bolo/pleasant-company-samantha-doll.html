Pleasant Company Samantha dolls: outfit and era matter

Conservative price signal: Four clear auction or fixed-price full-doll sales averaged $95.75 before shipping. Ordinary tan-body dolls vary with outfit and condition; early white-body examples are premium outliers.

Sample: 4 full-size Pleasant Company Samantha dolls sold Aug. 17–Sep. 27, 2026; best-offer and white-body premiums excluded.

Exact identification cues

Samantha has brown eyes and long brown hair with bangs. Check the neck stamp for Pleasant Company, confirm the doll is 18 inches tall, and identify whether the torso is tan cloth or the scarce early white cloth. The original meet outfit includes the checked dress, tights, shoes, ribbon and historically appropriate accessories.

Condition and testing

Check limb tightness, silver eye, eyelash loss, hair cuts or dryness, ink marks, odors and torso staining. Photograph the neck stamp and hair length. Missing shoes, tights or ribbon reduce a supposed complete meet outfit.

Buy and listing plan

Try to pay no more than: $35 for an ordinary tan-body doll in usable condition; up to $55 when the original meet outfit is substantially complete
Suggested listing price: $109.99–$129.99 for a clean complete ordinary example
Quick-sale target: $79.99–$99.99

Shipping plan

Protect the face and eyes with clean tissue, secure the hair without tight bands, wrap the limbs, and use a sturdy 20-inch-class box. Do not let accessories rub the vinyl.

Main risks

White-body, signed or early transitional dolls can be worth much more and require separate research. Haircuts, loose limbs, silver eye and mixed reproduction clothing can cut value sharply.
