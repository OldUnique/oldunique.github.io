Vintage patterned Pyrex: what to check before buying

Working price signal: Common collectible pieces often sit around $30–$80, while complete Pink Gooseberry or Turquoise Butterprint sets can reach roughly $200–$600 depending on completeness and condition.

Fast identification checklist

Look for bright white opal glass, a fired all-caps PYREX backstamp, glossy color, and a recognized pattern. Butterprint and Gooseberry remain easy patterns to remember, but the exact bowl number and colorway matter.

Condition changes everything

Pass or sharply reduce your offer for dishwasher haze, faded graphics, chips, rim roughness, deep scratches, or mismatched lids. Complete nesting sets deserve a premium only when every bowl is the correct size and pattern.

Buy and listing plan

For an ordinary single piece, keep the buy price near $15 or less unless current sold results support more. Start around $49.99 for a clean desirable piece, then adjust by exact pattern, number, lid, and condition.
