Pendleton Beaver State blankets: labels, size and value

Conservative price signal: Full-size named-pattern examples in the reviewed sample sold from $163 to $475.99. Use $150–$350 as a cautious working band until the exact pattern, size and condition are known.

Sample: 4 full-size or lap-robe fixed-price/auction sales plus several size variants checked Sep. 25, 2026.

Exact identification cues

Look for a sewn Pendleton label naming “Beaver State,” “Robes and Shawls,” or Pendleton Woolen Mills, and photograph the pattern name or stock tag when present. Measure both dimensions. Do not price a 24×41 saddle blanket like a 64×80 robe.

Condition and testing

Unfold fully under bright light. Check both sides for moth holes, edge loss, stains, odors, blanket-stitch damage and dry-cleaning shrinkage. Original box or tags help only if the textile itself is clean.

Buy and listing plan

Target buy ceiling: $60 for a clean full-size named pattern; $25 or less when pattern/size is uncertain
Suggested list price: $199.99–$349.99 after exact pattern research
Quick-sale target: $149.99–$249.99

Shipping plan

Fold with clean tissue, place in a clear protective bag, then use a sturdy box. Weigh after packing; wool blankets are bulky and dimensional weight matters.

Main risks

Pattern, size and provenance drive large price swings. Small saddle blankets, pillows and damaged examples are not comparable to full-size robes. Best-offer listings may hide the true accepted price.
