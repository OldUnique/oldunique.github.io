Sony WM-FX cassette Walkmans: tested units to hunt

Conservative price signal: Six recent ordinary tested WM-FX units averaged $42.55 before shipping; a practical typical band is about $35–$50. Premium Japan-made and serviced models can be outliers.

Sample: 6 ordinary tested sales

Exact identification cues

Confirm SONY and the WM-FX model number on the front, belt clip area, or battery compartment. Look for a cassette door plus AM/FM controls. Record the exact suffix: WM-FX141, FX195, FX241 and FX403 do not price identically.

Condition and testing

Test play, fast-forward, rewind, radio, volume wheel, headphone jack, battery terminals, door latch and speed stability. Belt slip, battery corrosion and channel dropout are common. “Radio works” does not prove the cassette mechanism works.

Buy and listing plan

Try to pay no more than: $15 tested working; $5 untested unless the model is clearly premium
Suggested list price: $49.99–$59.99 for a clean ordinary tested unit
Quick-sale target: $34.99–$44.99

Shipping plan

Remove batteries. Wrap the player, then box it with 2 inches of cushioning; avoid a soft mailer. State whether headphones, clip and case are included.

Main risks

Unknown belt condition, corrosion under the battery cover, speed drift, fake “tested” claims and model-to-model price variation. Treat the $168 WM-FX833 comp as an outlier, not the baseline.
