ANIMATED HALLOWEEN DECOR · PRICES CHECKED OCTOBER 2, 2026

Telco Motion-ette witches: test every movement

Conservative price signal: Six comparable Telco witches produced a $43.57 displayed-price average before shipping, with ordinary working examples clustering roughly from $44 to $70 and incomplete or weaker examples selling lower.

Sample: 6 Telco witch sales from Sep. 20–30, 2026 across roughly 19.5- to 24-inch versions; a suspicious $700 seller result, Gemmy pieces and unrelated smaller figures excluded.

Exact identification cues

Look for Telco branding and “Original Motion-ettes of Halloween” wording on the base, tag or box. Match the year, height and props: pumpkin, skull, broom and illuminated head vary by version. Do not substitute similar Gemmy or unbranded animated witches.

Condition and testing

Run the motion, light and sound through several cycles. Listen for grinding gears, watch for intermittent movement, and inspect thin plastic hands, face, broom and pumpkin for cracks. Check clothing, hair and wiring for storage damage.

Buy and listing plan

Try to pay no more than: $12 unboxed but fully working; up to $20 for a clean boxed example
Suggested listing price: $54.99–$69.99 for an ordinary tested version
Quick-sale target: $39.99–$49.99

Shipping plan

Immobilize moving arms and loose props without tying against brittle plastic. Support the figure inside a tall box, protect the face and hands, then double-box if the original carton is included.

Main risks

Version, height and working features materially affect price. Old gear trains can fail after testing. A $700 completed result from a zero-feedback seller was excluded as an unreliable outlier.
