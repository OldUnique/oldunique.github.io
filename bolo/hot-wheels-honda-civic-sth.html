Confirm it is the Super Treasure Hunt

Match the exact green Honda Civic Custom, premium-style finish, wheels, card art, and Super Treasure Hunt cues. The ordinary mainline version is a different item with a much lower value.

Card condition matters

Collectors pay more for a clean blister, flat card, crisp corners, and no creases or hanger damage. Photograph the card front, back, corners, blister seal, and the car’s finish.

Buy and listing plan

Try to pay $25 or less. A clean carded example can start around $89.99, with $74–$84 as a faster-sale range. Recheck recent sold listings because new-release prices can move quickly.
