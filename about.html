ABOUT FLIP SIGNAL

A shorter, calmer way to hunt for profitable finds.

Flip Signal publishes three focused resale leads each week. The goal is not to overwhelm you with hundreds of maybes. It is to give you a small, useful shortlist you can remember while visiting thrift stores, estate sales, auctions and local pickups.

How items make the list

Each signal is selected using recent market evidence, identifiable product details and a realistic margin between the target purchase price and likely selling price. We prioritize completed or demonstrated sales over ambitious asking prices.

What the numbers mean

Prices are snapshots, not guarantees. Condition, completeness, fees, shipping and demand can change the result. Always confirm the exact model or pattern and check the newest sold listings before spending money.
